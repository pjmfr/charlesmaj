The Charleston — Quick Reference

In American Mahjong, the Charleston is a series of tile exchanges before play begins. Remember the sequence with R · O · L · L · O · R (Right, Over, Left … Left, Over, Right), then an optional Courtesy Pass.

Blind Pass: On the last pass of each Charleston you may pass 1–3 tiles from those you just received, face-down, without looking.
Courtesy Pass: Only with the player across; agree on 0–3 tiles — the lower number wins.
